## Review questions

- What was incorrect?
- What was incomplete?
- Which validation rule was missed?
- Which step consumed the most time?
- Which manual action can be standardized?
- Which error could have affected the final result?
- What will be changed in the next attempt?

This is a personal practice scorecard, not an official competition scoring scheme.
